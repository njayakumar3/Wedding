## Hall Rules (St Augustine Hall)

- **Seating:** 160 people at 20 round tables (8/table) with dance floor open; 224 at 28 tables with dance floor closed
- **Decorations:** Tables and free-standing only. Nothing attached to walls/doors/windows.
- **Prohibited:** Confetti, glitter, rose petals, rice, spray foam, birdseed, anything that stains or is hard to clean.
- **Candles:** Must be dripless and in approved holders. Open flame OK with that constraint.
- **DJ/performers:** Must be approved by the parish.
- **Alcohol:** Allowed, but off-duty police officer required the entire event ($60/hr).
- **Kitchen:** Licensed caterers must complete parish kitchen training at least 30 days before and be present during event. Business license and certificate of insurance required.
- **A/V booth:** Off-limits.

---

## Church Rules (Ceremony)

- **Church available:** 90 min before ceremony (11:30 AM for 1 PM wedding)
- **Photos after:** 30 min max (must clear before 4 PM vigil Mass)
- **No receiving line** (due to time constraints with 4 PM vigil Mass)
- **Rooms:** Faith Formation building rooms available 90 min prior
- **Wedding party:** Arrive at least 30 min before ceremony
- **Ushers:** Arrive dressed, gather in narthex for boutonnieres
- **Rehearsal:** 45 min max
- **Photography:** From back of church only during ceremony, no flash, photographer must be dressed formally
- **Videography:** Set up 30 min prior at back of church, cannot move during ceremony, no floodlights
- **Flowers:** Church provides 2 altar arrangements (default white). No aisle runners. Nothing thrown. Nothing on the altar. Decorating complete 30 min before ceremony. Remove non-staying decorations within 30 min after photos.
- **Attire:** No bare shoulders/back (shawl/bolero for strapless). Hem below knee. Applies to wedding party too.
- **No alcohol** before ceremony on church grounds.
